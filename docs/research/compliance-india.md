# Compliance Notes — India

Status: Phase 0 research. **Not legal or tax advice.** Every item marked ⚖️ needs review by a lawyer or chartered accountant before launch.

## 1. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025 ⚖️

- Rules were notified in November 2025 with phased commencement; most fiduciary obligations apply after a transition period (verify exact dates against the Gazette notification).
- **Roles**: the _school_ is the Data Fiduciary for its students/staff data; **we are a Data Processor** acting on the school's instructions. Our contract (DPA) with each school must say so.
- **Children (under 18)**: the Act requires verifiable consent of a parent/guardian before processing a child's data, and prohibits tracking/behavioural monitoring and targeted advertising directed at children.
- **School exemption**: the Rules' Fourth Schedule exempts educational institutions from the verifiable-consent and no-monitoring obligations _only for processing tied to educational activities and the child's safety_ (including transport providers tracking children for safety). Use outside that purpose (marketing, analytics for third parties) is not covered.
- **Product implications**
  - No advertising, no behavioural profiling, no sale/sharing of student data — ever.
  - Purpose-limited data model; optional fields off by default (blood group, Aadhaar, caste category only where a board/government form needs it).
  - Record guardian consent/acknowledgement of the school's privacy notice (who, when, version) even where exempt — cheap and defensible.
  - Notices in English + Hindi + regional languages (configurable templates).
  - Rights: access, correction, erasure (subject to school's legal retention), grievance contact per school.
  - Breach response: notify school (fiduciary) without delay so it can notify the Data Protection Board and affected people; keep an incident runbook.
  - Retention schedule per data class; deletion jobs; tenant data export for portability.

## 2. UDISE+ / SDMS, PEN and APAAR

- Schools report school, teacher and student data to UDISE+ annually "as on" a reference date (typically 30 Sep). The student module captures 60+ fields per student.
- **PEN** (Permanent Education Number, 11 digits) is generated in UDISE+ SDMS; **APAAR ID** (12 digits) is generated by the school from the UDISE+ record after parental consent. NCERT has stated APAAR is not mandatory for admission.
- **Product implications**: store `pen` and `apaar_id` as optional verified identifiers on the student; map our fields to the UDISE+ data capture format; provide an export that matches it. We do **not** call UDISE+ systems directly (no public API).

## 3. Education boards

- CBSE, CISCE (ICSE/ISC), 30+ state boards, IB, Cambridge. Assessment schemes, grading bands and report-card formats differ and change by circular.
- **Product implications**: assessment schemes, grade bands and report templates are data (per tenant, per class range), seeded with templates we maintain; never hard-coded.

## 4. GST on school fees ⚖️

- Education services by a school up to higher secondary to its students are GST-exempt; services _to_ such schools like student transport, catering, security/housekeeping, and admission/exam-related services are also exempt.
- Exemption depends on who is the recipient: a vendor billing students directly may not get it.
- **Product implications**: fee heads carry a `tax_category` (default exempt); support optional taxable heads (e.g. uniforms/books sold by school, coaching) with GSTIN on receipt if the school is registered. Validate with a CA.

## 5. Payments ⚖️

- Use an RBI-authorised Payment Aggregator (Razorpay/Cashfree/PayU). We never touch card data (PCI scope stays with the gateway).
- Settlement goes to **the school's own bank account** (sub-merchant / route model) — we must not hold school money, which would raise PA licensing issues.
- Webhooks verified by signature; reconciliation against settlement reports.

## 6. SMS (TRAI DLT)

- Every commercial SMS in India must come from a DLT-registered entity, with registered header (sender ID) and an approved content template; template ID must be sent with each SMS. Variable-tagging rules were tightened in Jan 2026.
- Each school (or we, as entity) must register; entity registration ~₹5,000/yr + GST on some portals.
- **Product implications**: `notification_templates` store DLT template ID + header per tenant; onboarding checklist for DLT.

## 7. WhatsApp

- Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform via a BSP; only pre-approved templates outside the 24-hour window; collect opt-in.

## 8. Employment & payroll ⚖️

- PF (EPF Act, ≥20 employees), ESI (wage ceiling), Professional Tax (state-specific slabs), TDS on salary, gratuity, Shops & Establishment / state school education acts.
- **Product implications**: payroll rules configurable per tenant and state; ship as "assisted calculation", require CA sign-off of templates.

## 9. Accounting & records ⚖️

- Schools are often trusts/societies; audit needs head-wise receipts and concessions. Many use Tally for books.
- **Product implications**: our ledger is a fee sub-ledger, not full books; provide Tally-compatible export. Retain financial records ≥ 8 years (verify).

## 10. Other

- RTE Act: 25% seats in private unaided schools for disadvantaged groups — record admission category and fee reimbursement status.
- POCSO / child safety: restrict who can see child contact details and photos; audit access.
- Data residency: no law currently requires school data to stay in India, but some buyers will ask. Neon has no India region (Singapore is closest) — revisit if this blocks sales.
